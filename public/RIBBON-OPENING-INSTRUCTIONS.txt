REALISTIC RIBBON OPENING ASSET
==============================

Place the final pre-composed opening video in this folder with this exact name:

  ribbon-opening.mp4

Recommended export:
- Format: MP4
- Codec: H.264
- Canvas: 1080 x 1920 (9:16)
- Frame rate: 30 fps
- Duration: 3 to 5 seconds
- Audio: AAC, optional
- First frame: must visually match invitation-cover-v2.png or the wrapped-card poster
- Last frame: must visually match the first visible frame of the website hero/cover
- Web optimized / fast start: enabled
- Target file size: ideally 2 to 6 MB

IMPORTANT:
Do not use a green-screen video directly in the browser. Composite the ribbon over the actual invitation cover in Blender, DaVinci Resolve, After Effects, or another editor, and then export a normal MP4.

The website already:
- starts the video after a user tap
- supports inline playback on iPhone
- unlocks scrolling when the video ends
- includes a Skip opening button
- stops playback when the opening is skipped
- handles missing, blocked, interrupted, or stalled playback
- falls back to a simple cover crossfade if the video is missing or blocked
- respects reduced-motion settings
